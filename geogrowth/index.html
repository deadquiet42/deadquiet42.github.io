Geogrowth

a geometric art tool

A geometric art tool exploring pattern, preference and perspective. Choose how many squares, circles, lines, semicircles and dots to use, how much they vary in size and rotation, and how heavy the stroke is, then generate.

Invert, Colour and Glitch change how each composition is drawn. When one is worth keeping, download it.

Browser

Open Geogrowth

Runs in any modern browser. Download saves the current composition as a 4096 by 4096 pixel PNG.

Notes

Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Geogrowth is freeware. Your compositions are yours to keep and share.
