LILT

a musical grid

A musical grid for small computers. Place notes on a sixteen by eight grid and let them loop, then shape the sound with echo, space, pace and wobble across nine voices in three sound banks.

Every sound is generated as it plays. Headphones are worth it.

Handheld (PortMaster)

lilt.zip

18 KB · In testing.

Install on a handheld

  1. You need a Linux handheld with PortMaster installed.
  2. Copy lilt.zip, still zipped, into the autoinstall folder inside PortMaster on your SD card.
  3. Open PortMaster once. LILT installs itself and appears under Ports.

Controls

D-padmove (hold to keep moving)
Aplace notes (hold to paint)
Bremove notes (hold to erase)
Xempty the pattern
Ychange wobble
L1 and R1change pace
L2 and R2change voice
Select and left or rightchange echo
Select and up or downchange space
Startrest or resume
Select and Xfresh start
Select and Startexit

Notes

A small native program using the system SDL2 library. No runtime and no bundled files. Made for 640 by 480 screens; it letterboxes on others.

LILT is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
