MOMO

a small creature

A small electronic creature that lives in your handheld. The device is its body, and the screen is where its eyes are.

Prompt it, tease it, or leave it be; it reacts, chatters in its own voice, and dozes when ignored. Give it five minutes rather than thirty seconds; it takes a little while to show its character.

Handheld (PortMaster)

momo.zip

4.6 MB · In testing.

Install on a handheld

  1. You need a Linux handheld with PortMaster installed.
  2. Copy momo.zip, still zipped, into the autoinstall folder inside PortMaster on your SD card.
  3. Open PortMaster once. MOMO installs itself and appears under Ports.

Controls

Achange eye colour
B, X, Ystartle, play, watch
D-padsurprise, soothe, tease, greet
L1 and R1nudge from the left or right
L2 and R2poke from the left or right
Left stickdraw its attention, where present
Right stickedge, lean in and withdraw, where present
Select and Startexit

Notes

Pure Python on PortMaster’s python_3.11 runtime. Its voice is generated by its own code and shipped as a small bank of sound files. Made for 640 by 480 screens; it letterboxes on others.

MOMO is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
