MOMO

a small creature

A small electronic creature that lives in your handheld. The device is its body and the screen is where its eyes are. It notices you, reacts, chatters in a voice of its own, gets bored, dozes, dreams and remembers you between visits.

Every MOMO is born different, with its own voice, eyes and colour, and it gets to know you over days rather than minutes. Give it time: leave it alone, stroke it, tap it a rhythm, then come back later.

Handheld (PortMaster)

momo.zip

50 KB · In testing. Source

Install on a handheld

  1. You need a Linux handheld with PortMaster installed.
  2. Copy momo.zip, still zipped, into the autoinstall folder inside PortMaster on your SD card.
  3. Open PortMaster once. MOMO installs itself and appears under Ports.

Controls

Aboop
B, X, Ystartle, play, watch
X, tappedtap a rhythm and MOMO sings it back
D-padsurprise, soothe, tease, greet
L1 and R1nudge; hold to stroke
L2 and R2poke from the left or right
Left stickdraw its attention, where present
Right stickedge, lean in and withdraw, where present
Select, heldrepaint its colour
L1, R1 and Start, heldreset: let go after 5 seconds and it forgets you; hold for 10 seconds for a new MOMO
Select and Startexit

Notes

Pure Python on PortMaster’s python_3.11 runtime. Its voice is synthesised as it speaks; there are no sound files. Made for 640 by 480 screens; it letterboxes on others.

MOMO is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
