STAK

a balancing game

Choose from three shapes, turn and place each one, and balance six pieces into a structure that stands on its own.

It is harder than it looks. A run to five is good.

Handheld (PortMaster)

stak.zip

22 KB · In testing.

Install on a handheld

  1. You need a Linux handheld with PortMaster installed.
  2. Copy stak.zip, still zipped, into the autoinstall folder inside PortMaster on your SD card.
  3. Open PortMaster once. STAK installs itself and appears under Ports.

Controls

D-pad up and downchoose shape
D-pad left and rightmove shape
L1 and R1turn shape (hold to keep turning)
Adrop or confirm
Bcancel
Startrestart
Hold Selectreset best score
Select and Startexit

Notes

A small Python program using PortMaster's shared Python runtime and the system SDL2 library. No bundled files; every sound is generated at launch. Made for 640 by 480 screens; it letterboxes on others. Your best score is kept between plays.

STAK is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
