STAK

a physics sculpture

Choose from three shapes, turn and place each one, and balance six pieces into a structure that stands on its own.

It is harder than it looks. A run to five is good.

These builds are unsigned, so your system may warn you the first time. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. On macOS, open the app once, then allow it in System Settings under Privacy and Security. On Linux, a 64 bit desktop is needed; if STAK won't start, mark the file as executable.

Notes

Made in PICO-8, then carried over to handhelds and desktops in Python. Your best score is kept between plays.

STAK is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
