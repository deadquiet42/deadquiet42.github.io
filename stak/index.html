STAK

a physics sculpture

Choose from three shapes, turn and place each one, and balance six pieces into a structure that stands on its own.

It is harder than it looks. A run to five is good.

Cartridge

stak.p8.png

19 KB · For PICO-8, or any handheld with a PICO-8 player. The cartridge is the picture; open it in PICO-8 with load stak.p8.png.

Controls

Up and downchoose shape
Left and rightmove shape
Hold O, then left or rightturn shape
Xdrop or confirm
Ocancel
Pause menurestart, reset best score

On a keyboard, O is Z and X is X. Press Enter for the pause menu.

Notes

Made in PICO-8. Your best score is kept between plays.

STAK is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
