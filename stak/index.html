STAK

a physics sculpture

A physics sculpture for small computers. Choose from three shapes, turn and place each one, and balance six pieces into a structure that stands on its own.

It is harder than it looks. A run to five is good.

Handheld (PortMaster)

stak.zip

26 KB · Tested on RG35XXSP (muOS) and R36S (ArkOS).

Desktop versions

Windows Linux

The same piece for computers, with Python built in. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown developer; it isn’t signed, because signing costs money every year. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Install on a handheld

  1. You need a Linux handheld with PortMaster installed.
  2. Copy stak.zip, still zipped, into the autoinstall folder inside PortMaster on your SD card.
  3. Open PortMaster once. STAK installs itself and appears under Ports.

Controls

D-pad up and downchoose shape
D-pad left and rightmove shape
L1 and R1turn shape (hold to keep turning)
Left stickchoose and move, where present
Right stickturn, where present
Adrop or confirm
Bcancel
Startrestart prompt
Hold Selectreset best score prompt
Select and Startexit

Keyboard

Up and down arrowschoose shape
Z and Xturn shape
Spacedrop or confirm
Backspacecancel
Rrestart
Hold Deletereset best score
Escexit

A game controller also works on desktop.

Notes

Pure Python on PortMaster’s python_3.11 runtime. No bundled libraries, fonts or sounds. Made for 640 by 480 screens; it letterboxes on others.

STAK is freeware. You are welcome to share unmodified copies.
